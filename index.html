🚶 Getting here from Sumiyoshi Station

  1. Take the Tokyo Metro Hanzomon Line or the Toei Shinjuku Line to Sumiyoshi Station.
  2. Leave the station toward Shin-ōhashi-dōri (the main avenue that runs above the station).
  3. It's a short, flat walk of just 2 minutes to the venue. The Google Maps button above will guide you door-to-door.
